## Portfolio item 2

**Title:** DiskPilot — safety-first disk and USB toolkit (C#/.NET + PowerShell)

**Description:**

Three small, safety-first disk tools for USB sticks and data disks, published on GitHub. DiskPilot is a conservative partition manager (CLI plus Avalonia desktop GUI, C#/.NET) that calls the operating system's own storage tools and never writes raw disk data itself. USBImager captures a USB stick to an image and burns it back at full capacity. USBDoctor diagnoses USB drive problems read-only, decides who is at fault (drive, port, PC, or permissions), and offers only the repair that is safe for that state.

**Link:** https://github.com/caeled/DiskPilot
